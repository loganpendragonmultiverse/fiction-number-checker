# Interpretation guide

- A review group means the same subject label and fact type appeared with more than one normalized value.
- It does not account for time, uncertainty, dialogue reliability, hypothetical statements, flashbacks, or unit precision.
- Explicit subject filters reduce accidental grouping and are recommended for serious review.
- Context excerpts remain copyrighted manuscript content and should not be published accidentally.

English number words zero–ninety-nine are recognized only in the documented age, explicit duration and focused count contexts. Unsupported larger, signed, decimal and conjunction phrases are not partially normalized. This remains syntactic evidence for human review.
